What an immobilizer actually is

An immobilizer is an electronic vehicle security system that checks a key or transponder before allowing the vehicle to start. The exact design differs by vehicle. A replacement key may need to be cut and paired with the vehicle’s key system.

When a key is presented, the vehicle checks whether it is recognized by the system. If it is not recognized, the vehicle may not start. The exact behavior depends on the vehicle.

On vehicles that use a transponder, cutting the blade alone may not complete the job. Ask about programming for the specific Proton model and year.

What happens when you turn the key

  1. 1

    The key is presented

    Depending on the vehicle, a key or smart key communicates with the vehicle’s security system.

  2. 2

    The system checks the key

    The vehicle checks whether the key is programmed for that system. Key technologies differ between models and years.

  3. 3

    The vehicle responds

    If the key is not recognized, the vehicle may not start. A fault can have other causes, so symptoms alone cannot confirm an immobilizer problem.
