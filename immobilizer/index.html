The immobilizer chip inside your Proton key

Every Proton built since the early 2000s carries a small transponder chip in the key. Without it the engine will not start, even with a perfectly cut key. Here is what the chip does, why a hardware shop key will never work on its own, and how we program a new one in Kuching.

What an immobilizer actually is

The immobilizer is an anti-theft system built into the car's engine computer. Inside the plastic head of your key sits a transponder — a tiny glass or carbon chip with no battery of its own. It is powered by a coil of wire wrapped around the ignition barrel, or by the antenna ring in a push-start car.

Turn the key and the car asks the chip a question. The chip answers with a code. If the code matches what the engine computer expects, fuel and ignition are released and the car starts. If it does not match, the starter may crank but the engine will not fire.

This is why cutting a key alone is never enough on a modern Proton. A cut key will open the door and turn in the ignition, but the car stays immobilised until the chip is programmed to that specific vehicle.

What happens when you turn the key

  1. 1

    The coil wakes the chip

    The antenna ring around the ignition sends out a radio field. The transponder has no battery — it draws its power from that field.

  2. 2

    The chip answers

    It transmits its encrypted ID back to the immobilizer module. The code changes on every attempt on newer systems, so it cannot simply be copied off the air.

  3. 3

    The ECU decides

    The engine computer compares the answer against the keys stored in its memory. Match, and fuel injection is enabled. No match, and the engine is blocked.

Signs of an immobilizer or chip fault

Kereta tak boleh start? These are the usual symptoms.

Engine cranks but will not fire

The starter turns normally but the engine never catches. The classic immobilizer block.

Security light keeps blinking

The key symbol or red dot on the dashboard flashes instead of going out after ignition on.

Starts, then dies after two seconds

Fuel is cut a moment after firing because the chip was not recognised in time.

One key works, the spare does not

Usually a damaged transponder in the spare, or a key that was cut but never programmed.

Proton immobilizer questions

Can a normal key cutter make a Proton key?

They can cut the blade, and it will open the door. But without programming the transponder to your car's immobilizer, the engine will not start. Cutting and programming are two separate jobs.

I lost all my keys. Is that still possible to fix?

Yes. All key lost is a normal job for us. Bring your identity card and proof of ownership such as the grant or insurance, and we will produce a working key on site.

Will programming a new key erase my existing keys?

On a spare key job your current keys keep working. On an all key lost job the old keys are wiped from memory, which is what stops a lost key from ever starting your car again.

A spare key is usually under 15 minutes. All key lost takes longer depending on the model and the immobilizer generation.
